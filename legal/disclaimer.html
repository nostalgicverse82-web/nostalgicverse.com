Archival Disclaimer

Legal statements regarding digital preservation fair use, intellectual property attributions, and historical verification methodology.

1. Fair Use & Digital Preservation

NostalgicVerse is a non-profit preservation initiative. Game covers, hardware photographs, screenshots, and trademarked names are exhibited under fair use doctrines for educational, historical analysis, and archival preservation.

2. No ROM Distribution

NostalgicVerse does not distribute, host, or link to copyrighted commercial ROM files, game disk images, or proprietary system BIOS files.

3. Factual Verification

While every effort is made to guarantee strict historical accuracy through cross-referencing primary sources, historical records from early gaming eras can occasionally conflict. Corrections accompanied by primary documentation are welcomed.
