Genre Families of Interactive Play

Action

Mechanical reflex, spatial precision execution, collision detection, and physical timing. Governs platformers, fighters, brawlers, and stealth infiltration.

Shooter

Spatial projectile trajectories, viewport perspective, and fast-paced ranged engagements. Spans first-person masterworks, shmups, and battle royales.

Adventure

Environmental puzzle solving, ability-gated progression, narrative stakes, and world discovery. From Metroidvanias to survival horror and interactive fiction.

Role-Playing

Character attribute growth, moral choice architecture, strategic party building, and sprawling questlines derived from tabletop traditions.

Strategy

Macroscopic resource management, positional tactics, base construction, and high-level analytical decisions in real-time or turn-based pacing.

Simulation

Systems-thinking sandboxes, emergent social modeling, municipal planning, and physical consistency modeling real-world or speculative systems.

Racing & Sports

High-velocity racing line optimization, vehicle physics tuning, drafting dynamics, and digital recreation of real-world athletic competition.

Puzzle & Logic

Pattern recognition, spatial geometry manipulation, auditory beat synchronization, and unhurried intellectual problem solving.
