The North American Debut of the Sony PlayStation

On September 9, 1995, Sony released the PlayStation in North America at an iconic $299 price point. Backed by dedicated 3D geometry rendering and high-capacity CD-ROM architecture, the machine permanently transformed gaming from a toy aisle commodity into a dominant mainstream medium.
