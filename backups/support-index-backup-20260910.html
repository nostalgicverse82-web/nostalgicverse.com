Help ensure that the early decades of interactive software and hardware remain documented, accessible, and accurately remembered for future generations.

How to Support Preservation

• Archival Submissions: Contribute verified technical manuals, high-resolution hardware photography, or primary developer documentation.
• Error Correction: If you identify a factual discrepancy in release dates or hardware silicon specifications, contact our editorial desk.
