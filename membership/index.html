The NostalgicVerse Ethical Preservation Charter

Unlike modern commercial web portals, NostalgicVerse was created as a permanent, non-commercial cultural sanctuary. We believe that gaming history is cultural heritage that belongs to humanity. Here is our binding pledge:

No Paywalled History
The entire 500+ game catalog, 65+ console technical specs, and timelines will always remain 100% free to read for every visitor on earth.
Zero Surveillance Ad-Farms
No deceptive download buttons, pop-under redirects, video auto-play, or third-party behavioral tracking. We only accept verified preservation partners.
Archival Reinvestment
100% of memberships and donations fund physical hardware acquisition, scan calibration, server bandwidth, and historical documentation.
